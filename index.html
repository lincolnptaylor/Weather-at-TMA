Round & Estimate

Dew point: 36.518 rounds to 37

I rounded to the nearest whole number. The digit after the decimal point is 5, and 5 is 5 or more, so I rounded up to 37.

Temperature: 51 rounds to 50

I rounded to the nearest ten. The digit in the ones place is 1, and 1 is 4 or less, so I rounded down to 50.

The average for dew point and temperature is about 37 for the dew point and about 50 for the temperature.

Data Story

The temperature was bigger than the dew point on 10 of my 11 days. The mean temperature was 51 and the mean dew point was 36.518. One thing that surprised me is that on one of the days, the dew point was equivalent to the temperature.

The Day the Temperature Hit the Dew Point

Out of my 11 days, one stood out. On 10/25 at 7:53 in the morning, the temperature was 30°F and the dew point was also 30°F. This was the only day they matched, which meant the humidity was 100%.

The dew point is the temperature where the air gets so full of water that it can’t hold any more. When the temperature drops to the dew point, water comes out of the air as dew. Since it was 30°F, which is below freezing, the water would have turned into frost instead.

This makes sense because it was early in the morning after a cold night, and it was also my highest air pressure day. High pressure usually brings clear, calm nights that let things cool down a lot. On every other day, the temperature stayed above the dew point, so the air never filled up all the way.
